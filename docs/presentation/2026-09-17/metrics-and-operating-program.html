What the report can—and cannot—say

The boundary keeps DWARF an adversarial real-node framework instead of an inflated benchmark claim.

Technically defensible

  • Relative protocol goodput on pinned real nodes
  • Latency and recovery degradation under named faults
  • Cardano/Amaru differential under identical conditions
  • Resource cost per measured unit of useful work
  • Exact assertion, transcript, trace, and observer evidence

Not legitimate

  • Global Cardano TPS or production capacity
  • Adoption inferred from socket or HTTP acceptance
  • Exact saturation without a controlled rate sweep
  • Production CPU efficiency from Antithesis
  • Internal causation for an uninstrumented stage
  • Bias-free results without paired stock runs
